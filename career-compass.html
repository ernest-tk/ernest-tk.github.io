Answer 12 quick statements about yourself. You will get three careers to explore, plus the subjects that usually lead to them.

Your top three to explore

These are starting points, not decisions. Your interests can grow, and many careers need skills you can still build.

Think it through

  1. Which skills for your top career do you already have? Which will you build, and how?
  2. Talk to someone who works in it. What does an ordinary day look like?
  3. Which of your subjects will you need to work hardest at to keep this door open?
