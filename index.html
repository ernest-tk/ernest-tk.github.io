About me

I teach Physics and Principal Mathematics to A-Level students and develop curriculum materials under the NCDC framework. My aim is for learners to become creative, critical thinkers and problem solvers, and good exam results follow from that.

I start from everyday intuition and build up to the formal method. I also build interactive tools that let students see an idea working before they write it down.

What I teach

A-Level Physics

Senior Five and Senior Six topics, from mechanics and circular motion to gravitation, with worked examples and problem-solving practice.

Principal Mathematics

Clear methods for Senior Five and Senior Six, with step-by-step solutions and common mistakes pointed out.

How I teach

  1. Begin with something students can picture or try.
  2. Move step by step to the formal rule or equation.
  3. Finish with problems that make learners apply the idea in new situations.
